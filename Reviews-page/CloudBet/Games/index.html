Cloudbet Casino Game Rules and Fairness

Cloudbet's general betting terms include provisions governing accepted bets, betting limits, settlement, technical errors, account restrictions, and RNG-based games.

RNG Testing

Under Clause 10.30 of Cloudbet's supplied terms, casino, slot, and other RNG-based games offered on the website use random number generators independently tested for fairness and randomness by accredited testing laboratories.

The same clause distinguishes RNG games from live dealer and other non-RNG products, whose outcomes and disputes are subject to the applicable settlement and error provisions.

Betting Limits and Accepted Bets

Clause 10.15 states that if Cloudbet erroneously accepts a bet exceeding the stated maximum amount, the excess is disregarded, the stake is revised, and the difference is returned.

Under Clause 10.8, an accepted bet cannot be amended, withdrawn, or cancelled by the player.

Technical Errors and Settlement

Clause 10.24 explains that a bet is confirmed when it reaches Cloudbet's servers. If communication is interrupted after the bet has been received, it remains active. If the bet is not received, it does not participate and the stake is not deducted.

Clause 10.25 requires players to notify Cloudbet in writing about betting-related disputes within 30 days of the transaction. The clause also establishes the operator's system transaction logs as the determining record in the event of a discrepancy.

Account and Betting Restrictions

Cloudbet's terms prohibit activities intended to circumvent betting limits, account restrictions, verification requirements, or promotional rules. Clauses 10.28 and 10.29 address account transfers, use of another player's account, coordinated activity across multiple accounts, and related enforcement measures.

Players should review the applicable game rules and current betting limits before placing wagers.
