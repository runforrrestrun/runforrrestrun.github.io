These testing procedures are intended to assess whether the games operate according to their specified random outcome mechanisms.

Return to Player Monitoring

Cloudbet distinguishes between theoretical RTP and actual RTP when explaining its game monitoring procedures.

Theoretical RTP represents the expected percentage of total wagers returned to players over an extended period of gameplay.

Actual RTP measures the returns observed during a particular period of real gameplay.

Cloudbet states that it conducts quarterly analyses to compare actual game returns against theoretical RTP values.

However, RTP is a statistical measure calculated across gameplay activity and does not guarantee a particular return to an individual player.

Live Casino Fairness

Live dealer games are also subject to statistical performance checks. Unlike RNG-based games, their outcomes involve physical equipment and human dealers.

Cloudbet Whale Mode

Whale Mode is an automated position-building feature designed for players who want to place larger bets.

This functionality is particularly relevant to high-volume sports bettors, although the final amount placed depends on market conditions and available betting opportunities.
