Wazamba Withdrawal Processing Time

The reviewed cryptocurrency withdrawal options displayed a processing estimate of 1–3 days. Wazamba's Terms provide additional detail, stating that withdrawal requests are worked through by the financial department within three business days after the request is submitted and/or three business days after the previous withdrawal request has been paid, provided the applicable requirements and checks have been completed.

The financial department processes withdrawal requests from 6 AM to 5 PM GMT, Monday to Friday. Once Wazamba has processed the transfer request, further delays caused during payment processing are outside the company's stated responsibility.

Withdrawals can also take longer when additional verification is required. Wazamba may delay a payment while checking the player's identity, account balance, source of funds or compliance with its Terms.

Wazamba Withdrawal Limits

Wazamba applies both payment-method-specific limits and general account withdrawal limits. According to the Terms, the general minimum withdrawal is €10 per transaction and the maximum withdrawal amount is €20,000 per month.

A player can have no more than three pending withdrawals at the same time. Wazamba also reserves the right to establish an individual payment schedule and change the minimum, maximum or processing time of a transaction according to the withdrawal method, account level or other relevant factors.

This means the €20,000 monthly maximum should not be confused with the maximum amount shown for an individual payment transaction. Bank Transfer and Skrill displayed maximums of €5,000, MiFinity showed €2,500, and each of the reviewed cryptocurrency withdrawal options showed a €5,000 maximum.

Wazamba Wagering Requirements Before Withdrawal

Wazamba requires deposited funds to be wagered before they can be withdrawn. The minimum funds rollover is 1x the deposit amount.

If the amount wagered is lower than the deposit amount, Wazamba reserves the right to cancel winnings and apply a commission of 10% of the last deposit, with a minimum charge of €0.50. The Terms specify a higher 15% commission when the account balance was funded by bank card or bank transfer.

Wazamba also states that deposited funds and subsequent winnings are subject to a minimum monthly wagering requirement before a withdrawal can be approved. The company can decline a withdrawal if the player has not reached a minimum wagering threshold of €20 during the calendar month in which the withdrawal is requested.

Wazamba KYC and Payment Verification

Wazamba can perform additional checks before processing a withdrawal. These checks can cover the player's identity, account balance, source of funds and compliance with the casino's Terms.

If additional verification is required, Wazamba can postpone payment until the relevant checks have been completed. Players are required to cooperate with these investigations and provide the requested information.

Payment ownership can also be checked in connection with refunds. Wazamba may request an identity document, proof of address and verification that the player owns the payment method used on the account.

Wazamba Refund Policy

Wazamba generally does not provide refunds for deposited funds, although it may consider a refund in exceptional situations such as technical problems involving the Website or payment instruments.

A refund cannot be made after the relevant deposit or an associated bonus has already been used to place a bet. To request consideration for a refund, the player must contact customer support within 24 hours of the transaction. Wazamba states that it will endeavour to respond within 10 business days, although refunds remain at the company's discretion.

Verification can be required before a refund or reverse transaction is completed. Requested documents can include an identity document, proof of address and proof of payment-method ownership. If the requested documentation is not provided within 30 days, the refund or reverse transaction may not be completed.

Are Player Funds Protected at Wazamba?

Wazamba states that customer funds are kept separately from company funds in dedicated client bank accounts. According to its Terms, this arrangement is intended to protect customer balances in the event of company insolvency, with the funds held in player accounts to be returned to customers.

The Terms also clarify that a Wazamba account balance is not a bank balance. Deposited funds do not earn interest and are not treated as an insured bank deposit.

Wazamba Inactive Account Fee

An account can be classified as inactive if the account holder does not log in for 180 days. Wazamba may then charge a €5 monthly administration fee while the account remains inactive.

If the remaining balance is below €5, the entire balance can be charged. No further inactive-account fees apply once the balance reaches zero.
