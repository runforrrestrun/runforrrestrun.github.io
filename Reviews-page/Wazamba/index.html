Is Wazamba Safe?

Wazamba has several account-security and compliance procedures described across its Privacy Notice, AML information and account-verification documentation. Wazamba Registration and Account Information

Wazamba's Privacy Notice identifies several pieces of information that may be collected when creating and operating an account. Registration data can include an email address, date of birth, country, phone number, gender, full name, social security number, ZIP code and username.

What Data Can Wazamba Collect?

In addition to registration and KYC information, Wazamba can collect device information, IP addresses, operating-system information, device identifiers, usage logs, session activity and general location information.

How Can Wazamba Use Personal Data?

The supplied Privacy Notice lists purposes including providing the service, answering requests, developing and improving products, preventing fraud and illegal activity, fulfilling regulatory obligations, resolving disputes, maintaining platform security and conducting marketing where applicable.

Wazamba also states that gaming behaviour can be analyzed to identify potential harmful behaviour, manage risks, support responsible gaming and recommend games or features.

Who Can Wazamba Share Data With?

Depending on the purpose, personal data can be shared with authorized employees and related entities, payment providers, financial institutions, technology and customer-service providers, fraud-prevention agencies, game providers, analytics providers, regulatory authorities and law-enforcement agencies.

Payment providers can receive information necessary to process transactions and comply with their own legal and AML obligations. The Privacy Notice states that this can include personal information and, where necessary, KYC documentation.

How Long Does Wazamba Keep Personal Data?

Wazamba states that personal information is retained while the player maintains an account and for the purposes described in its Privacy Notice. Information can be retained for longer where legal obligations, including AML requirements, require it.

Once an account is closed and there is no longer a legal or business reason to retain the information, Wazamba states that personal data will be deleted or anonymized so it can no longer be associated with the player.

Player Privacy Rights
