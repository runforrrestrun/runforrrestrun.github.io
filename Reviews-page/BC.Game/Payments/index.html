Payment transparency could be improved by presenting complete fee information and withdrawal maximums more consistently. The separate withdrawal fee explanation refers to a minimum 0.1% charge, while the general terms allow other disclosed fees and third-party network costs. This makes checking the final cashier quotation particularly important.

Overall, BC.GAME provides broad payment flexibility, especially for cryptocurrency users. Nevertheless, higher withdrawal thresholds, variable processing conditions and incomplete fee information for some methods mean that players should review the exact transaction requirements before depositing or requesting a withdrawal.
