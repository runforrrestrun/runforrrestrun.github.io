ThunderCrash

ThunderCrash is an original crash game built around a spacecraft that rises as an on-screen multiplier increases.

Players place a wager before the spacecraft takes off. The multiplier begins at 1.0x and increases while the spacecraft continues flying. Players must cash out before the spacecraft crashes to receive a payout based on the multiplier reached.

If the spacecraft crashes before a player cashes out, the wager is lost.

The supplied game description also mentions visual elements appearing during the flight, including bananas and ticking bombs.

ThunderCrash Auto Cashout

ThunderCrash includes an Auto Cashout feature. Players can select a multiplier, such as 2.0x, at which the game will attempt to cash out automatically.
