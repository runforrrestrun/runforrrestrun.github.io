Account Security

BetFury recommends two concrete steps to lock down an account, and puts the responsibility for both mostly on the player:

  • Use a strong, unique password: a mix of upper and lower case letters, numbers, and special characters, not reused from any other site or service.
  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA): set up in a few minutes from the Account page using an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator or Authy. Save the recovery code somewhere safe when you activate it, since it's the only way back in if you lose access to the authenticator.

2FA is worth treating as mandatory rather than optional, particularly for protecting your linked email and any connected exchange account.
