Single bets are straightforward wagers on one outcome. Parlays, also called accumulators, combine multiple selections into a single bet with a higher potential payout, but every selection has to win for the parlay to pay out, which raises both the risk and the potential reward. Live betting lets you place wagers on matches already in progress, with odds shifting in real time as the action unfolds, so a missed pre-match opportunity doesn't have to mean missing out entirely.
