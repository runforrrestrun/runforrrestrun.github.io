7Bit Casino RTP and Slot Volatility

Return to Player (RTP) and volatility are two important characteristics when comparing casino games.

RTP represents the theoretical percentage of total wagers that a game is designed to return over a very large number of rounds. It does not predict the outcome of an individual session.

For example, a game with a 96.5% RTP is designed to return an average of €96.50 for every €100 wagered over the long term, leaving a theoretical house edge of 3.5%.

Volatility describes how frequently a game tends to produce payouts and how those payouts are distributed.

Casino Game RTP and Volatility Explained
Feature Meaning
RTP The theoretical percentage of total stakes returned over long-term gameplay.
Low Volatility Typically associated with more frequent, smaller payouts.
High Volatility Typically associated with less frequent payouts and greater potential payout variation.
Maximum Win The highest advertised payout permitted by a game's rules.
House Edge The theoretical long-term advantage retained by the game operator.
