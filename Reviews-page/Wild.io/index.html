Responsible Gambling at Wild.io

Wild.io provides responsible gambling controls designed to help players manage their activity. Its policies emphasise that gambling should remain entertainment rather than a source of income.

Players can access the Personal Limits section of their accounts to configure restrictions. The available tools include:

  • Loss limits: Restrict losses over a selected daily, weekly, or monthly period.
  • Wager limits: Restrict the amount wagered over a selected period.
  • Session limits: Restrict the amount of time spent gambling.
  • Cooling-off periods: Temporarily prevent deposits and participation in promotions.
  • Self-exclusion: Disable account access for a selected period.

There are important differences between these controls. Wild.io states that loss, wager, and session limits currently apply to the casino section, while cooling-off and self-exclusion restrictions apply to both the casino and sportsbook.

Decreasing a personal limit takes effect immediately. Increasing a limit may require email confirmation and the expiry of the previous limit period.

Wild.io also provides educational material covering gambling warning signs, budgeting, self-assessment, and support resources. Its responsible gambling information refers players to independent organisations including GamCare, Gamblers Anonymous, and the National Council on Problem Gambling.
