Wild.io Originals Dice, Mines and Limbo

Dice, Mines and Limbo offer different forms of player-controlled risk. In Dice, players adjust their target probability and choose whether the result should fall above or below a selected value. Mines requires players to reveal safe tiles while avoiding hidden mines, with potential payouts increasing as more safe tiles are uncovered.

Limbo uses a target multiplier instead of a reel or grid. Players choose a multiplier before the round begins, and a result meeting or exceeding that target produces a payout. Higher target multipliers correspond to lower probabilities of success.

What Does RTP Mean at Wild.io?

Return to Player, or RTP, is the theoretical proportion of wagers a game is designed to return over a sufficiently large number of rounds. For example, a game with 96% RTP theoretically returns 96 units for every 100 units wagered over the long term.

This does not mean a player wagering $100 will receive $96 back. Individual sessions can produce substantially different results, including the loss of the entire amount wagered.

Game Volatility and House Edge

Volatility describes how game payouts are distributed. Lower-volatility titles generally have smaller, more frequent payouts, while higher-volatility games may produce longer periods without significant wins and less frequent large payouts.

The house edge is the mathematical advantage built into a game. Cryptocurrency payments do not change the underlying house edge or theoretical RTP of a casino title.

However, using cryptocurrencies whose market prices fluctuate introduces a separate financial consideration: the fiat value of a player's balance can change independently of gambling results.
