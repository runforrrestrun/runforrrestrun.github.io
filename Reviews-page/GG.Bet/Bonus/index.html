Cash and Bonus Balance

GG.BET separates player funds into a Cash Balance and Bonus Balance. Deposited cash and eligible cash winnings form the Cash Balance, while promotional funds and bonus-derived winnings remain in the Bonus Balance until the wagering conditions are completed.

Cash funds are used first. Only after the available Cash Balance has been wagered does GG.BET begin using the Bonus Balance.

Attempting to withdraw before completing the applicable wagering requirements results in the bonus balance and associated winnings being forfeited.

Only One Active Bonus

GG.BET permits only one active bonus at a time. Activating another bonus can deactivate or pause the previous promotion depending on the applicable offer conditions.

This matters particularly for Deposit Quest, Marathon and other progress-based promotions because activating another offer can interrupt the player's progress.

Game Contribution to GG.BET Wagering

Not every casino game contributes equally toward wagering. GG.BET states that some games and providers are completely excluded, others contribute 50%, and instant games contribute only 10%.

Casino bonuses cannot be wagered through live casino or table games. Players using a slot promotion should therefore check whether their selected game contributes fully before attempting to clear the wagering requirement.

GG.BET Free Spins Rules

Free spins are frequently tied to specific slots. Gates of Olympus 1000, Gates of Olympus Super Scatter and Joker Stoker are among the games attached to the promotions covered above.

If the designated free-spin game is unavailable because of regional restrictions, currency compatibility or another availability issue, GG.BET allows players to contact Customer Support for a replacement game where applicable.

Free-spin winnings should not automatically be treated as withdrawable cash. Promotions can impose separate wagering and maximum-conversion rules. For example, the €300 deposit promotion has 40× wagering and limits conversion from free spins to three times the winnings with a €150 ceiling.

GG.BET Bonus Abuse Rules

GG.BET prohibits using multiple accounts to claim promotions repeatedly, misusing free bets, manipulating bonus mechanics and deliberately using gameplay strategies designed primarily to clear wagering requirements.

Players are also prohibited from postponing bonus rounds or free spins in an attempt to move their value between bonus and real-money balances.

If GG.BET determines that a promotion has been abused, it reserves the right to cancel the bonus, confiscate associated winnings and restrict the player from future promotions.

GG.BET Bonuses Verdict

GG.BET has an unusually broad bonus structure, particularly for players who continue depositing after registration. The recurring promotions add considerably more variety. Deposit Quest is suited to smaller repeated deposits, while the €300 challenge and €1,000 Marathon target much higher deposit activity. Players interested in the current offers should check the available GG.BET bonuses before depositing and compare the wagering, validity and cashout conditions rather than choosing an offer based only on its headline percentage.
