GG.BET Maximum Withdrawal

There is an important distinction between the maximum for an individual payment method and GG.BET's overall payout limit.

GG.BET Payment Fees

GG.BET does not provide enough consistently visible cashier information for us to assign a specific withdrawal fee to every reviewed payment method. We therefore do not consider it appropriate to label all GG.BET withdrawals as free.

The Payment Policy also states that charges associated with receiving funds through Bank Transfer are borne by the recipient. This means players using Bank Transfer should account for possible receiving or intermediary banking costs.

There is another potentially significant charge connected to GG.BET's deposit rollover rules. If a player attempts to withdraw after meeting the minimum 1× rollover but before reaching 2× qualifying wagering, GG.BET reserves the right to deduct a 20% administrative fee, subject to a minimum of $0.50 or equivalent.

GG.BET Deposit Rollover Before Withdrawal

GG.BET requires deposited funds to be wagered before they can be withdrawn. According to the payment rules, players must complete at least 1× their deposit before a withdrawal can be processed.

There is an additional distinction at 2× wagering. If the total amount staked is less than 2× the deposited amount, GG.BET reserves the right to charge the 20% administrative fee described above.

GG.BET KYC and Payment Verification

Players should not treat GG.BET as a guaranteed no-KYC casino. Its AML/KYC policy establishes several points at which identity verification becomes required.

Verification is required when whichever applicable threshold comes first: within 30 days of the first deposit, once cumulative deposits reach €2,000, or before the first withdrawal. This makes the first withdrawal particularly important because players can be required to complete KYC even if they have not deposited €2,000.

GG.BET can request information and documentation to establish identity, address and payment ownership. Its policies also allow additional checks where required for AML, fraud prevention or other due-diligence purposes.

Payment Method Ownership

GG.BET requires payment accounts and wallets used for withdrawals to belong to the player. A withdrawal can be restricted or rejected if it appears to be directed to another person's account or wallet.

The company may also request proof that the player owns the relevant payment method. This is particularly important when verification is required before processing a withdrawal or refund.

GG.BET Withdrawal Processing Times

GG.BET does not provide one fixed withdrawal processing time that applies to every transaction.

The Terms state that processing times depend on regulatory procedures and the company's internal requirements and are determined case by case. Additional checks can also take place within non-fixed timeframes.

For that reason, we would not describe GG.BET withdrawals generally as instant based on the information available to us. The actual time can depend on the payment method, verification status and any additional checks applied to the transaction.

Can GG.BET Use a Different Withdrawal Method?

Yes. GG.BET states that although it aims to use the player's preferred payment option, it cannot guarantee that the requested method will always be used.

The company can process a withdrawal through an alternative payment system when necessary. Withdrawal requests must also be submitted through the Website; requests made by phone, email or live chat are not accepted.

GG.BET Currency Conversion

GG.BET states that currency conversion is not available on the site. If funds are deposited in a particular currency, games requiring a different currency may not be accessible with that balance.

Players who want to change their account currency are instructed to contact Customer Support. This makes choosing the appropriate account and deposit currency important before funding the account.

GG.BET Refund Policy

GG.BET generally does not refund deposited funds. Refunds may be considered in exceptional circumstances, including certain technical problems involving the Website or payment instruments.

A deposit or associated bonus becomes ineligible for a refund once it has been used to place a bet. A refund request must be submitted to customer support within 24 hours of the relevant transaction, and GG.BET states that it aims to respond within 10 business days.

Refunds remain discretionary and can be withheld while identity or payment ownership is verified. If documentation is requested, players can be required to provide it within 30 days. Bonuses and winnings are deducted before the final refundable amount is calculated where a refund is approved.
