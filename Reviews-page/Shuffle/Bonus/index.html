How Shuffle Calculates Bonus Wagering

Unless the individual offer states otherwise, Shuffle calculates the wagering requirement using the eligible deposit and the awarded bonus.

Wagering Formula: (Eligible Deposit + Deposit Bonus) × Wagering Multiplier

For example, consider a hypothetical promotion with the following conditions:

  • Eligible deposit: $100
  • Deposit bonus: 100%, equal to $100
  • Wagering requirement: 35×

The calculation would be:

($100 + $100) × 35 = $7,000

In this example, the player must complete $7,000 in qualifying wagering progress before the bonus-related funds become eligible for withdrawal.

This is an illustration of the wagering formula, not confirmation that Shuffle currently offers a permanent 100% bonus with a 35× wagering requirement.

Shuffle Bonus Wagering Contribution

Not all wagers contribute equally toward a deposit bonus wagering requirement.

Unless the promotion states otherwise, Shuffle uses the theoretical house edge of the eligible game to calculate wagering progress.

Wagering Progress = Wager Amount × (Game Edge ÷ 4%)

For example, a $10 wager on a game with a theoretical house edge of 1% contributes:

$10 × (1% ÷ 4%) = $2.50

Therefore, the $10 wager contributes $2.50 toward the wagering requirement rather than the full amount wagered.

This system means games with different theoretical house edges may contribute at different rates. Certain games, providers, or betting features may also be excluded entirely.
