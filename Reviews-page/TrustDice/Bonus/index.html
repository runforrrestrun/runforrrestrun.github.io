Wagering Requirement

The confirmed figure, straight from each deposit bonus's own Bonus Rules panel, is 40x — and importantly, it's calculated on your deposit plus the bonus combined, not the bonus alone. For example, a $10 deposit with a $20 bonus (1st or 3rd deposit tier) creates a combined total of $30, so the wagering requirement is $30 × 40 = $1,200 in total bets before the bonus and any winnings become withdrawable.

Payout Caps and Bonus Validity

Once wagering is complete, the maximum amount released to your withdrawable balance is capped at 10x the deposit bonus amount, or a fixed currency limit, whichever is lower. For EUR, that fixed cap is 1,000 EUR; for USDT, it's 1,000 USDT (figures vary by currency — check the Bonus Rules panel for your specific currency). You have 7 days from receiving the bonus to complete the wagering requirement, or the bonus and any winnings are forfeited.

While wagering the deposit bonus, there's also a maximum single bet size that still counts toward the requirement — roughly 2 EUR or 2 USDT equivalent (again, currency-specific). Betting above that size on a single wager won't count toward clearing the bonus.

The following do not contribute toward wagering: voided or pushed bets, cashed-out bets, system bets, unsettled bets, hedging or betting on both sides of the same market. Bets placed using a risk-free bet or the freebet_refund, freebet_freemoney or freebet_no_risk offers are also excluded.

How Claim Points and Daily TXT Rewards Work

Every real-money bet generates Claim Points, win or lose. These points determine your share of that day's released TXT:

Claim Points = Bet Amount (USD) × Game Percentage × (1 + Bonus Percentage) × 20

Game percentage used in the Claim Points formula
Game TypePercentage
Dice1.5%
Crash1.5%
Sports3%
Casino3%

At 09:00 UTC daily, that day's Play-to-Earn round closes and TXT is distributed based on each player's share of total Claim Points, then every player's Claim Points reset to zero. For example, if there's 2,500,000 TXT to distribute on a given day and total Claim Points across all players is 1,000, a player holding 10 Claim Points would receive (10 × 2,500,000) ÷ 1,000 = 25,000 TXT.

Bonus Percentage (Boosting Your Claim Points via Staking)

The "Bonus Percentage" in the formula above starts at 0% and increases based on how much TXT you have staked before 08:00 UTC the previous day. A higher Bonus Percentage means your bets generate Claim Points faster.

Staking TXT for Daily Dividends

Separately from the Bonus Percentage mechanic, staked TXT also qualifies for a share of daily dividends paid from a staking pool. Worth flagging: TrustDice's own site states the staking pool size two different ways in different places — as 50% of Dice game profits in one description and 10% of Dice game profits in another, more detailed section. Similarly, the daily distribution time is given as both 09:00 UTC and 10:00 UTC in different spots. Until you confirm which is current directly on the Stake tab of the TXT page, don't present either figure as the definitive one. What is consistent across both versions: 1% each of EOS, BTC, ETH, and USDT from the pool is distributed daily to TXT holders in proportion to their staked amount, and only staked TXT qualifies.

While staked, TXT can't be used to play games, tip other players, or be withdrawn. To access it again, you first click "Unstake," wait 72 hours, then perform a separate "Refund" action to return the tokens to your usable balance.
