TrustDice Provably Fair System

One of the more distinctive parts of TrustDice's crypto-focused offering is its use of provably fair verification for supported Originals. Cryptographic information such as server seeds, client seeds, nonces and hashes can be used to verify completed game results.

The purpose of provably fair technology is verification, not prediction. It allows a player to check whether a recorded result corresponds with the cryptographic data used by the game system, but it does not reveal the next result, eliminate the house edge or provide a way to guarantee a profit.

The distinction is also important because provably fair verification applies to supported TrustDice Originals rather than automatically applying to every third-party slot, live dealer table or sportsbook market available through the platform.
