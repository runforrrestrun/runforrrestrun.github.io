TrustDice Payments Review

TrustDice offers a broad payment system covering both traditional EUR payments and cryptocurrency transactions. Players can fund their accounts with cards, bank transfers and several e-wallets, deposit supported cryptocurrencies directly, or use the integrated Buy Crypto service to purchase crypto with EUR.

The reviewed cashier also provides several withdrawal routes, including cryptocurrency, Instant SEPA, standard SEPA, MiFinity and Jetonbank. Deposit fees are particularly competitive, with all reviewed EUR deposit methods showing a 0% fee, while withdrawal costs vary according to the selected method and cryptocurrency network.

This TrustDice Payments review looks at the available deposit and withdrawal methods, minimum and maximum limits, fees, processing times, supported crypto networks and the Buy Crypto options. The figures below are based on the TrustDice cashier reviewed for this page, so payment availability and limits may vary depending on location, currency and the payment provider.

TrustDice Deposit Methods

TrustDice divides its funding options into three main categories: traditional fiat deposits, direct cryptocurrency deposits and Buy Crypto. This gives players a choice between depositing EUR directly, transferring crypto from an external wallet, or purchasing crypto through an integrated third-party payment service.

Fiat Deposits

The TrustDice EUR cashier supports six direct fiat deposit methods. All of the methods reviewed showed a 0% deposit fee and an instant estimated processing time.

Vega has the lowest minimum deposit at €10, while the other reviewed methods start at €15. For larger deposits, Jetonbank stands out with a €100,000 maximum, and no maximum amount was displayed for Bank Transfer in the reviewed cashier.

The combination of low minimums and zero displayed deposit fees makes the fiat cashier suitable for both smaller deposits and higher-value transactions. Players using an e-wallet can choose between Revolut, MiFinity, Jetonbank and Vega, while Credit Card and Bank Transfer provide more conventional alternatives.

Fiat Deposit Processing

All six EUR methods were shown with an instant estimated processing time during the review. Actual processing can still depend on the payment provider, banking system or account checks, so the cashier's estimated processing time should be treated as an estimate rather than a guarantee.

The required network is particularly important for tokens such as USDT and USDC. The cashier specifically instructs users to send only the supported asset and network to the generated address. TrustDice requires a specific number of confirmations for each cryptocurrency before the deposit is credited to the account. The required number varies by blockchain and asset, as shown in the table above.

Crypto Deposit Requirements

  • Deposits below $0.10 may not be credited.
  • Only the cryptocurrency specified by the TrustDice deposit address should be sent.
  • The selected blockchain network must match the network shown in the cashier.
  • Internal transactions or unsupported contract deposits are not supported.
  • Users should check the address and network carefully before confirming a transaction.

The confirmation requirement differs between assets. BTC and TXT require one confirmation in the reviewed cashier, while TRX and XRP require 20. The stated credit time was usually within 30 minutes, although the actual time can depend on blockchain conditions.

Crypto Withdrawal Processing

The cashier states that crypto withdrawals are sent instantly in most cases. However, security checks, insufficient hot-wallet liquidity and other circumstances can delay a request for up to 48 hours.

The withdrawal amount must also be higher than the displayed gas fee. XRP withdrawals require both an XRP address and a Destination Tag.

TrustDice Payment Limits

Crypto withdrawals use cryptocurrency-specific limits rather than EUR-denominated limits, so the practical value of a maximum withdrawal depends on the asset's current exchange rate.

TrustDice Payments Verdict

rustDice provides a broad payment system covering both fiat and cryptocurrency transactions.

For EUR deposits, the cashier is particularly competitive. All six reviewed methods — Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Revolut, MiFinity, Jetonbank and Vega — showed 0% deposit fees, with minimums starting at €10. The limits also cover a wide range of use cases, from small deposits through Vega to a €100,000 Jetonbank maximum.

Crypto users have a similarly broad choice. BTC, ETH, USDT, USDC, LTC, DOGE, TRX, XRP and TXT can be deposited directly, while SOL is also available for withdrawals. The biggest consideration is network compatibility, particularly for assets such as USDT and USDC.

The Buy Crypto feature is one of the most flexible parts of the payment system. Instead of requiring players to purchase cryptocurrency elsewhere, TrustDice provides access to several third-party providers, including MoonPay, Banxa and Swapped. The available payment channels include cards, PayPal, bank transfer, SEPA, Google Pay, Apple Pay, Skrill, Neteller and PaySafeCard.

However, flexibility comes with an important caveat: different Buy Crypto providers can quote different amounts of cryptocurrency for the same EUR purchase. Checking the final amount received before confirming the transaction is therefore worthwhile.

On the withdrawal side, TrustDice offers both crypto and fiat routes. Crypto withdrawals do not carry a separate TrustDice withdrawal fee, although blockchain fees can apply. For EUR withdrawals, MiFinity has the lowest reviewed fixed fee, while Instant SEPA combines a high limit with instant estimated processing.

Overall, TrustDice offers a broad payment setup for both fiat and crypto players. Its main advantages are zero-fee EUR deposits, high limits on selected methods, a broad cryptocurrency selection and an integrated Buy Crypto system. The main things players need to watch are network compatibility, differences between Buy Crypto quotes and the fees attached to fiat withdrawals.
