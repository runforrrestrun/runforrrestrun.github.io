Roobet Payments Review 2026 – Deposits, Withdrawals, Fees & Limits

Roobet supports cryptocurrency deposits, fiat payment methods, direct transfers from supported exchanges and an integrated Buy Crypto feature. Players can fund their accounts using cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Tether (USDT), USD Coin (USDC), Solana (SOL) and several other digital assets. Fiat deposit options include Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Skrill.

Payment conditions vary depending on the transaction type. Fiat deposits start at €10 and are subject to a 1% deposit fee under Roobet's Terms and Conditions. Cryptocurrency purchases through the Buy Crypto feature start at €9, with provider fees ranging from 1.75% to 11% across the reviewed options. Transfers from exchanges and wallets have separate minimum amounts and supported blockchain networks.

Withdrawals are less transparent. During our review, Roobet did not display withdrawal methods, minimum or maximum limits, fees or estimated processing times because the account had no available balance. However, its Terms establish several important withdrawal conditions, including a deposit wagering requirement, identity verification and a 2% fee starting with the tenth fiat withdrawal within a rolling 30-day period.

Roobet provides multiple ways to fund an account, but the available options should not be confused with one another. Direct cryptocurrency deposits involve transferring existing crypto, fiat deposits use supported payment providers, and Buy Crypto allows players to purchase digital assets through a separate payment interface.

Roobet Deposit Methods

Roobet allows players to deposit through its Iron Pouch, the account balance system described in its Terms and Conditions. The cashier offers direct cryptocurrency deposits and fiat deposit methods, with additional options to purchase crypto or transfer assets from an external exchange.

Under Section 9 of the Terms, deposits are processed immediately by Roobet, although delays caused by payment providers are outside the operator's responsibility. The Terms also require players to use payment methods and cryptocurrency wallets that belong to them.

Section 10.2 also states that payment providers or Roobet may charge additional withdrawal fees, depending on the method used.

Roobet Withdrawal Verification Requirements

Under Section 10.5 of the Terms, Roobet may refuse to process a withdrawal until the player has completed the required checks.

  • Identity verification must be completed to Roobet's satisfaction.
  • The selected withdrawal method must be confirmed as belonging to the player.
  • Any additional requested documentation or information must be provided.
  • The minimum wagering requirement for each deposit must be satisfied.

Roobet may also investigate account activity before processing a withdrawal. If it reasonably suspects a breach of its Terms or applicable law, it may delay or decline transactions while conducting an investigation.

Do You Have to Wager Your Deposit Before Withdrawing From Roobet?

Yes. Section 10.1 of Roobet's Terms states that players must wager their deposited funds before withdrawing. Section 10.5 also makes completion of the minimum wagering requirement for each deposit a condition for withdrawal approval.

The supplied payment-related Terms do not state an exact wagering multiplier. Therefore, we cannot confirm whether the required amount is 1x the deposit or another value.

This condition applies to deposited funds and should not be confused with separate wagering requirements attached to casino bonuses.

Roobet Fiat Withdrawal Rules

Roobet's Terms require fiat withdrawals to use the same payment method used for the original deposit. If this is not possible, the operator may allow another payment method after the player provides proof of ownership.

When an account has been funded using several payment methods, Roobet reserves the right to divide a withdrawal between those methods.

The Terms also allow Roobet to reject multiple pending withdrawal requests and ask the player to submit one combined withdrawal instead.

Roobet Crypto Withdrawal Rules

Cryptocurrency withdrawals are sent to the wallet address provided by the player when submitting a valid withdrawal request.

Under Section 10.11, players are responsible for providing the correct wallet address. Roobet states that it is not liable for losses resulting from an incorrect or invalid destination address.

Players should verify the cryptocurrency, blockchain network and destination address before confirming any withdrawal.

Roobet Payments Review 2026 – Our Verdict

Roobet offers a flexible deposit system for players who use both cryptocurrency and conventional payment methods. Direct crypto deposits support major assets including BTC, ETH, USDT and USDC, while Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Skrill provide fiat deposit alternatives. The separate Buy Crypto and Mesh transfer features also make it possible to fund an account without relying on a single transaction method.

However, these options come with different costs. Successful fiat deposits carry a 1% fee, while Buy Crypto provider fees vary significantly. In the reviewed purchase interface, Apple Pay, Google Pay and cards displayed 1.75% fees, compared with 11% for PaySafeCard on supported purchases.

The main weakness is withdrawal transparency. Roobet did not display available withdrawal methods, limits, fees or estimated processing times while the account balance was empty. This makes it difficult to assess the complete payment experience before depositing. The published Terms also require deposited funds to be wagered before withdrawal and allow additional verification checks.

Overall, Roobet provides a broad selection of deposit and crypto purchasing options, but its withdrawal conditions are less transparent than its deposit features. Players should review all applicable charges, network requirements and withdrawal restrictions before funding their accounts.
