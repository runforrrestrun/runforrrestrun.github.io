Are Roobet Originals Provably Fair?

Roobet describes its Originals as using provably fair mechanics supported by random number generation. Provably fair systems allow players to check whether an outcome corresponds to the underlying verification data.

This feature is different from Return to Player (RTP). Provably fair verification concerns the integrity of a particular result, while RTP describes the theoretical long-term payout percentage of a game.

How Roobet Predictions Works

Roobet Predictions uses a share-based system where prices represent the market's estimated probability of an outcome. For example, a share priced at $0.45 represents an implied probability of 45%. Prices can change as participants trade or new information becomes available.

Unlike conventional sports wagers, prediction market positions can be sold before an event concludes, provided sufficient market liquidity is available. However, selling early does not guarantee a profit, and low liquidity can make exiting a position difficult.
