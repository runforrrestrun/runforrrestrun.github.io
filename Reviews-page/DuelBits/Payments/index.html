DuelBits Payments Review 2026 – Deposits, Withdrawals & Fees

DuelBits supports cryptocurrency deposits and withdrawals, with ten cryptocurrencies available in the cashier we reviewed. Players can use Bitcoin, Ethereum, Litecoin, Solana, Dogecoin, XRP, BNB, Tron, USD Coin and Tether. The platform also provides Buy Crypto and Gift Cards sections, alongside 16 currencies for displaying account balances.

Withdrawal minimums vary by cryptocurrency and blockchain network. In our cashier review, the lowest displayed minimum was €8.90, while Bitcoin required at least €44.50. Estimated transaction fees also differed significantly between networks.

This DuelBits Payments Review 2026 examines the available deposit and withdrawal methods, minimum withdrawal amounts, estimated fees, currency conversion, processing policies and refund conditions. All amounts shown below reflect the cashier information available during our review and may change.

Which DuelBits Cryptocurrency Has the Lowest Withdrawal Minimum?

In our review, Litecoin, Solana, Dogecoin, XRP, Tron and USDC on Solana had the lowest displayed withdrawal minimum of €8.90.

Ethereum, BNB, USDC on ERC-20 and all three listed USDT networks required at least €22.25. Bitcoin had the highest displayed minimum at €44.50.

These figures demonstrate why the selected cryptocurrency and network matter when planning a withdrawal.

USDC and USDT Withdrawal Networks

DuelBits provides more than one network option for selected stablecoins.

USDC withdrawals were available through ERC-20 and Solana. The Solana option had a lower displayed withdrawal minimum of €8.90, compared with €22.25 for ERC-20.

USDT withdrawals were available through ERC-20, BEP-20 and TRC-20. All three displayed a minimum withdrawal of €22.25, but their estimated fees differed.

The largest estimated stablecoin withdrawal fee in our snapshot was 4.68575140 USDT (€4.17) for USDT on TRC-20. This was considerably higher than the estimated fees displayed for USDT on ERC-20 and BEP-20 at the time of our review.

Players should compare the currently displayed fees and confirm that their receiving wallet supports the selected network before requesting a withdrawal.

How to Withdraw from DuelBits

  1. Sign in to your DuelBits account and open the cashier.
  2. Select the withdrawal option.
  3. Choose the cryptocurrency and available blockchain network.
  4. Review the minimum withdrawal amount and estimated fee.
  5. Enter the required withdrawal information and follow the cashier instructions.
  6. Submit the withdrawal request and complete any required security checks.
  7. Wait for the transaction to be processed and confirmed.

Withdrawal requests are subject to DuelBits' original source rule, account verification requirements and applicable security checks. The company may delay processing when additional compliance reviews are necessary.

DuelBits Withdrawal Processing Times

DuelBits states in Section 7.3 of its Withdrawal & Remittance Policy that the company aims for near-instant withdrawal processing. However, it explicitly describes all withdrawal timeframes as indicative rather than guaranteed.

Withdrawals may take longer because of security reviews, compliance checks or blockchain congestion. The policy also states that the company does not accept liability for perceived or actual losses resulting from processing delays.

We could not establish a guaranteed withdrawal time for each cryptocurrency from the available cashier information. Players should therefore avoid assuming that every withdrawal will arrive immediately.

DuelBits Original Source Withdrawal Rule

Section 7.1 of DuelBits' Withdrawal & Remittance Policy requires withdrawals to be processed back to the original payment method or wallet used for the deposit.

This means players should not assume they can deposit through one wallet and freely withdraw to an unrelated wallet.

Section 7.2 provides an exception procedure for situations where a cryptocurrency wallet has been deactivated or lost, or a token has been delisted. In these circumstances, DuelBits requires enhanced verification before authorising an alternative withdrawal method.

Players who can no longer access their original wallet should contact customer support before submitting a withdrawal request to another destination.

How DuelBits Currency Conversion Works

According to the currency information displayed on DuelBits, all deposits are converted into internal DuelBits coins linked to the US dollar.

Players can select another currency to view an approximate local balance, but exchange-rate movements may create differences between displayed values.

DuelBits also warns that small variations in bet amounts and payouts can occur when changing currencies. These differences result from conversions involving values smaller than one US cent.

During our review, switching the account display currency did not change the USD pricing shown in the Buy Crypto and Gift Cards sections.

The availability of a display currency should not be interpreted as confirmation that DuelBits accepts direct bank deposits or withdrawals in that currency.

DuelBits Refund Policy

DuelBits publishes a refund policy covering purchases of on-site credits. The policy identifies Liquid Entertainment N.V., registration number 153298, as the operator and lists licence GLH-OCCHKTW076092020. The version supplied for this review was last revised on 26 August 2025.

Under the published refund conditions, a player may request cancellation within 14 days, provided the relevant credits satisfy the eligibility requirements.

Conditions for a Refund

The refund policy states that on-site credits must have been purchased within the applicable 14-day period and must remain unused.

Credits are not considered unused if they have already been wagered or transferred to another user. The policy also excludes credits that have become inseparably mixed with other items.

Eligible players can submit a cancellation request by emailing support@duelbits.com. The policy states that reimbursements are made no later than 14 days after the cancellation notice is received, using the original payment method without additional reimbursement fees.

The player remains responsible for the cost and risk of returning credits where a return is required. DuelBits may request proof that the relevant credits have been returned.

Important: This refund policy concerns eligible unused credits. It does not establish a right to recover gambling losses or bypass the standard withdrawal conditions.

Our DuelBits Payments Verdict

DuelBits provides a cryptocurrency-focused payment system with ten supported assets and 13 withdrawal options across the networks shown in our cashier review.

One advantage is the availability of relatively low withdrawal minimums for selected cryptocurrencies. Litecoin, Solana, Dogecoin, XRP, Tron and USDC on Solana all displayed minimums of €8.90.

However, the withdrawal experience depends on the selected asset and network. Bitcoin required a minimum of €44.50, while the estimated fee for USDT on TRC-20 was substantially higher than those shown for other stablecoin networks in our snapshot.

Players should also understand the original source rule, the possibility of verification-related delays and the distinction between display currencies and actual payment methods.

Overall, DuelBits offers several cryptocurrency payment options, but checking current minimums, network fees and withdrawal conditions before depositing is essential.
