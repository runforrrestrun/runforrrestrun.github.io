KatsuBet Welcome Pack Wagering Rules

All four Welcome Pack bonuses are subject to 35x wagering unless otherwise specified. KatsuBet also applies a specific maximum bet to these bonuses. The maximum per spin while wagering a Welcome Pack bonus is €1 or the listed equivalent in another supported currency.

This is important because KatsuBet's general bonus rules normally allow a maximum €5 bet unless another limit is stated. The Welcome Pack has its own lower limit, so the €1 rule applies while wagering these bonuses.

Welcome Pack bonuses expire after 14 days if the wagering requirement has not been completed. Real-money funds are used first when playing with an active bonus. Requesting a withdrawal of the deposited amount before completing wagering results in the bonus amount and associated winnings being forfeited.

KatsuBet also states that cancelling a Welcome Pack bonus, or selecting the option to refuse all bonuses, prevents the player from receiving the remaining Welcome Pack bonuses.

KatsuBet General Bonus Rules

KatsuBet limits customer offers to one per person, family, household address, email address, telephone number, payment account, IP and shared computer. Multiple accounts created to claim additional bonuses are considered bonus abuse, and funds connected to duplicate accounts can be forfeited.

Only one bonus can be claimed at a time, meaning deposit-related bonuses cannot be stacked. When a deposit bonus is accepted, the deposit becomes locked with the bonus. Players must either complete the wagering requirement or cancel the bonus. Cancelling results in the bonus and winnings attributed to it being lost.

When both real money and bonus funds are available, real money is used first. Bonus funds and winnings are also forfeited if a bonus expires before the applicable conditions are completed.

KatsuBet prohibits delaying game rounds, free spins or bonus rounds until after wagering has been completed or a new deposit has been made. The terms also prohibit using bonus funds solely to progress a game towards a bonus feature and then completing that feature later with cash funds.

Withdrawals are subject to an internal audit, and only fully settled wagers that result in a win or loss count towards wagering. KatsuBet also reserves the right to remove bonuses or associated winnings in cases involving bonus abuse, software exploitation or failed account audits.

KatsuBet No Deposit Bonus Rules

KatsuBet's terms contain a separate set of conditions for no-deposit bonuses and no-deposit free spins. However, the reviewed promotions supplied for this page did not include a standard registration no-deposit offer with a current fixed reward.

Where no-deposit free spins are issued, the general bonus terms state that they normally require 50x wagering unless otherwise specified. The maximum winnings from a no-deposit free-spin offer are €50, and winnings above that amount are forfeited.

KatsuBet can also request KYC before processing a withdrawal originating from a no-deposit bonus. The listed verification documents can include a government-issued photo ID and proof of residency such as a utility bill no older than three months.

KatsuBet Bonuses Verdict

KatsuBet has a larger bonus programme than its headline first-deposit offer suggests. The four-stage Welcome Pack provides up to €6,000 in combined maximum match bonuses and 200 free spins in EUR, while recurring Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and weekend promotions give existing players additional offers after the welcome sequence is finished. Daily Cashback, a separate crypto promotion, Wheel of Fortune, KatsuPoints and the eight-level VIP programme add further reward systems.

The main issue is the number of conditions attached to these offers. The standard Welcome Pack requires 35x wagering and restricts bets to €1, while the High Roller Bonus increases wagering to 45x. Other promotions use their own expiry periods, maximum winnings and wagering rules, and live games contribute nothing towards standard bonus wagering.

The strongest part of the programme is the amount of actual information available for each reward. Minimum deposits, codes, wagering requirements, maximum bets and many maximum-win limits are stated rather than hidden behind a single headline figure. Players still need to check eligibility and the rules of the individual promotion before activating it, particularly because KatsuBet applies extensive geographical restrictions to its bonuses.
