The payment setup has relatively low entry points, with the general KatsuBet terms stating a minimum deposit of €10 or equivalent and a minimum withdrawal of €20 or equivalent. However, the actual limits depend on the selected currency and payment method. This KatsuBet Payments review covers deposit and withdrawal methods, transaction limits, fees, processing times, supported currencies, crypto-to-fiat payments, KYC requirements and withdrawal rules. Method-specific figures are based on the KatsuBet payment information and cashier reviewed for this page, while availability can vary depending on currency and location.

KatsuBet Crypto-to-Fiat Exchange

One of the more distinctive parts of KatsuBet's payment system is Cryptoprocessing. It allows a player to fund a fiat-denominated casino balance using cryptocurrency rather than requiring the account balance itself to remain denominated in crypto.

For example, a player can select USD as the casino balance currency while depositing BTC. The Bitcoin is sent through Cryptoprocessing and converted so that the casino balance is credited in USD.

How KatsuBet Cryptoprocessing Deposits Work

  1. Sign in and open the KatsuBet Wallet or Deposit section.
  2. Select the fiat currency that will be used for the casino balance.
  3. Select a supported cryptocurrency through Cryptoprocessing as the payment method.

KatsuBet's supplied information states that the converted funds can appear on the casino balance within a few minutes, although actual crypto transaction times can depend on the payment process and network.

KatsuBet Payments Verdict

KatsuBet has a broad payment setup for players who want to use either fiat or cryptocurrency. Conventional options include cards, alternative payment services, Interac and Bank Transfer, while the reviewed crypto cashier supports BTC, ETH, USDT, LTC, DOGE, TRX, XRP, ADA, BCH and BNB. Cryptoprocessing adds further flexibility by allowing cryptocurrency to fund a fiat-denominated casino balance and supporting conversion in the opposite direction for withdrawals.

The current cashier is particularly important when checking limits because some figures differ considerably from KatsuBet's public Payments information. For example, the reviewed cashier showed maximum withdrawals of 20 BTC, 200 LTC and 75,000 USDT, compared with lower values in the general payment table. Fees also require attention: several fiat methods are free, while cards and Bank Transfer can carry a 2.5% charge, and current crypto fee information was not consistently displayed before the payment screen loaded.

Overall, KatsuBet provides considerable payment flexibility, but players should check the live cashier before each transaction rather than relying solely on general payment tables. The most important conditions to remember are the method-specific minimums, the warning that undersized crypto deposits will not be credited, the 3× general deposit wagering requirement or 10× requirement for table and live play, KYC at €2,000 in cumulative payments, and the overall €10,000 weekly and €40,000 monthly withdrawal limits.
